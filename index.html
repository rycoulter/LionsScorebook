      What To Track

      MLB-informed

      Plate appearance outcome: every PA powers AVG, OBP, SLG, OPS, wOBA-lite, strikeout rate, walk rate, and RBI opportunities.

      Pitch trail: counts, pitch volume, and first-pitch strike trends show approach quality before the result.

      Batted-ball profile: outcome, launch band, and location separate real damage from routine contact.

      Spray location: hit and out locations reveal pull tendency, opposite-field direction, and where defenses are taking away hits.

      Baserunning: steals, caught stealing, runs, and pressure speed influence lineup shape.

      Defense: positions and coach grades keep the best nine from becoming a fragile nine.
